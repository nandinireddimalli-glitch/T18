## 🎯 Objective

Understand how a marketing funnel works and how a customer moves from **first hearing about a product** to **paying for it** — and design the right message, content and call-to-action for each step of that journey.

---

## 📱 The Product: StudyNest

| | |
|---|---|
| **What it is** | An AI study planner app that turns a syllabus + exam dates into a daily, personalised study schedule |
| **Core features** | AI timetable generator, Pomodoro focus timer, syllabus progress tracker, smart revision reminders, streaks |
| **Pricing model** | Freemium — Free plan + **StudyNest Pro at ₹49/month** (student offer; ₹99 regular) |
| **Target market** | Indian college students (18–24), especially engineering & degree students during semester exams |
| **Unique Selling Point** | *"Upload your syllabus, get a plan in 30 seconds."* No manual timetable making |

> StudyNest is a sample product created for this exercise.

## 🔻 Marketing Funnel Diagram

![StudyNest Marketing Funnel](assets/funnel-diagram.png)

The funnel narrows as we go down: **many people** become aware, **fewer** seriously consider it, and **a focused group** converts. Intent increases at every stage, so the message gets more specific and the CTA asks for a bigger commitment.

| Stage | Customer's mindset | Our job | Commitment we ask for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Awareness** | *"I'm stressed and behind on studies."* | Get noticed, name the problem | Low — follow / watch |
| **Consideration** | *"I need a better system. What are my options?"* | Educate & build trust | Medium — free download / sign-up |
| **Conversion** | *"This works. Is Pro worth paying for?"* | Remove doubts, create urgency | High — start paid plan |

## 👤 Target Persona

**"Rahul, 20 — 3rd-year B.Tech student, Hyderabad"**

- **Goals:** Clear all subjects, keep CGPA above 8, have some free time
- **Pain points:** Starts studying too late, doesn't know where to begin, loses focus on phone, syllabus feels too large
- **Where he spends time:** Instagram Reels, YouTube (study & tech channels), WhatsApp class groups, Telegram notes channels
- **Buying behaviour:** Price-sensitive, trusts friends & seniors more than ads, needs to *try before paying*
- **Trigger moments:** 2–3 weeks before mid-sems/end-sems, result day, start of a new semester

## 👥 Audience Stage Definitions

### 1️⃣ Awareness — *The Unaware / Problem-Aware Student*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Who they are** | College students who feel exam stress and procrastinate but **don't know an app can fix it**. Not searching for a solution — just scrolling. |
| **What they think** | *"Why can I never finish the syllabus on time?"* |
| **What they need** | Relatable content that names their problem and makes them notice StudyNest |
| **Where to reach them** | Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, meme pages, campus ambassadors, college fests |
| **Stage goal** | Maximum reach & brand recall |

### 2️⃣ Consideration — *The Solution-Seeking Student*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Who they are** | Students who have **engaged with us** (followed, watched 50%+ of a video, visited the website) and are actively comparing study methods — Notion templates, paper planners, other apps. |
| **What they think** | *"Will this actually work for me? Is it better than what I use now?"* |
| **What they need** | Proof, demos, comparisons, reviews and a free, low-risk way to try |
| **Where to reach them** | YouTube long videos, Instagram carousels, website/landing page, retargeting ads, email |
| **Stage goal** | Build trust → get free app installs & sign-ups |

### 3️⃣ Conversion — *The Ready-to-Pay Student*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Who they are** | **Free users** who are active in the app (3+ sessions/week), and exams are close. They've seen value but haven't upgraded. |
| **What they think** | *"Is Pro worth ₹49? Can I wait?"* |
| **What they need** | A clear reason to upgrade **now** — urgency, a discount, a risk-free trial, social proof |
| **Where to reach them** | In-app banners, push notifications, email, WhatsApp broadcast, retargeting ads |
| **Stage goal** | Convert free users to Pro subscribers |

## 📝 Content for Each Stage

### 1️⃣ Awareness Content — *Talk about the problem, not the product*

| # | Format | Content idea | Platform |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Reel (15s) | *"POV: It's 2 AM before the exam and you just opened the syllabus"* — relatable meme ending with the StudyNest logo | Instagram |
| 2 | YouTube Short | *"3 reasons you forget what you studied (and how toppers fix it)"* | YouTube |
| 3 | Meme carousel | *"Types of students during exam week"* | Instagram |
| 4 | Infographic | *"The 25-minute rule: how the Pomodoro technique doubles focus"* | Instagram / LinkedIn |
| 5 | Campus activity | Campus ambassadors run a *"Study Streak Challenge"* poster + QR code at college fests | Offline |
| 6 | Collab | Micro-influencer (study / engineering creators, 10k–100k followers) shares their exam routine | Instagram / YouTube |

**Sample caption:**
> Syllabus: 5 units 📚 Days left: 3 😭 Brain: "Let's reorganise the desk first." 🧹
> Tag that friend who starts studying the night before 👇 #ExamSeason #StudentLife

### 2️⃣ Consideration Content — *Show how the product solves the problem*

| # | Format | Content idea | Platform |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Demo video (60–90s) | *"I uploaded my syllabus and StudyNest planned my whole semester in 30 seconds"* — screen recording | Instagram / YouTube |
| 2 | Comparison carousel | *"Paper planner vs Notion vs StudyNest"* — time to set up, reminders, auto re-planning | Instagram / LinkedIn |
| 3 | Lead magnet | **Free PDF:** *"The 30-Day Exam Prep Planner for Engineering Students"* (email required) | Landing page |
| 4 | Testimonials | Short video reviews from real student users — *"How I cleared 6 backlogs with a plan"* | Instagram / Website |
| 5 | Blog / SEO | *"How to make a study timetable for semester exams (with free template)"* | Website / Google |
| 6 | Email sequence | 3 emails after PDF download: tips → app walkthrough → student success story | Email |

**Sample caption:**
> Stop spending 2 hours making a timetable you'll never follow. ⏳
> Upload your syllabus → pick your exam date → StudyNest builds your day-by-day plan. Watch the full demo 👆

### 3️⃣ Conversion Content — *Give a reason to act now*

| # | Format | Content idea | Platform |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | In-app banner | *"Exams in 14 days — unlock AI revision mode with Pro"* | App |
| 2 | Push notification | *"Your 7-day free Pro trial is waiting 🎁 No card needed."* | App |
| 3 | Offer creative | **Exam Season Offer:** ₹49/month (50% off) — ends Sunday, countdown timer | Instagram / WhatsApp |
| 4 | Email | *"You've studied 12 hours with StudyNest. Here's what Pro adds…"* — personalised usage stats | Email |
| 5 | Retargeting ad | Ad shown to free users who visited the pricing page but didn't buy | Meta Ads |
| 6 | Trust content | FAQ graphic — *"Cancel anytime · Secure UPI payment · 4.6★ rated by students"* | Pricing page |

**Sample email subject lines:**
> - *"Rahul, your exam plan is 60% done — finish strong with Pro"*
> - *"Last 24 hours: 50% off StudyNest Pro 🔥"*

## 👉 CTA for Each Stage

The CTA grows in commitment as the customer moves down the funnel.

| Stage | Primary CTA | Secondary CTA | Why it fits |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Awareness** | **"Follow @studynest for daily study hacks"** | "Tag a friend who needs this" / "Save this for exam week" | Zero risk — we just want them to remember us |
| **Consideration** | **"Download StudyNest free"** | "Get the free 30-Day Exam Planner PDF" / "Watch the full demo" | Low risk — free value in exchange for attention/email |
| **Conversion** | **"Start your 7-day free Pro trial"** | "Upgrade to Pro at ₹49/month — offer ends Sunday" | High intent — urgency + risk-free trial removes the last doubt |

## 🔄 Message Progression (No Repeated Messaging)

Following the hint — *"Do not use the same message at every funnel stage"* — each stage answers a **different question** the customer has:

| Stage | Customer's question | Our message | Tone |
|---|---|---|---|
| Awareness | *"Why do I always struggle before exams?"* | **"Exam stress is a planning problem, not a you problem."** | Relatable, funny, empathetic |
| Consideration | *"How can I fix it?"* | **"StudyNest builds your study plan in 30 seconds — see how."** | Helpful, educational, proof-based |
| Conversion | *"Why should I pay now?"* | **"Unlock Pro before your exams — free for 7 days."** | Direct, urgent, reassuring |

❌ **Wrong approach:** Showing *"Buy StudyNest Pro now!"* to someone who has never heard of us.
✅ **Right approach:** Entertain → Educate → Offer.

## 📊 KPIs & Measurement

| Stage | KPIs | Monthly target | Tracking tool |
|---|---|---|---|
| Awareness | Reach, impressions, video views, follower growth, shares | 50,000 reach · 1,500 new followers | Instagram / YouTube Insights |
| Consideration | Link clicks, CTR, PDF downloads, landing-page visits, app installs | 3% CTR · 2,500 free installs | Google Analytics, Play Console, UTM links |
| Conversion | Trial starts, free → paid rate, revenue, cost per acquisition | 8% free → Pro · CPA under ₹60 | In-app analytics, payment dashboard |

**Funnel math (example):** 50,000 reached → 2,500 installs (5%) → 200 Pro users (8%) → **₹9,800/month** in new revenue at ₹49.

## 🎁 Bonus: Retention Stage

After conversion, keep users happy so they renew and refer friends:

- **Content:** Weekly progress reports, streak badges, "semester wrapped" summary
- **CTA:** *"Refer a friend — you both get 1 month of Pro free"*
- **KPI:** Renewal rate, referrals per user

---

## 💡 Key Learnings

1. **A funnel is a conversation, not one ad.** Each stage needs a different message because the customer is in a different state of mind.
2. **Commitment should rise gradually.** Follow → Free download → Paid plan. Asking for money too early kills trust.
3. **Know the audience at each stage.** Someone scrolling Reels needs entertainment; a free user near exams needs an offer.
4. **Every stage must be measurable.** Clear KPIs show where people drop off, so we know which stage to fix.
5. **Timing matters.** For students, the exam calendar is the strongest conversion trigger.
